# The Satzfunk family: voices, choirs and autonomous offspring

Satzfunk can become the audible publishing and listening home for many small **sing** instruments. “Babies” is an artistic metaphor for bounded, individually addressable instruments and performer agents—not an authorization for uncontrolled replication.

## Reusable composition

A child instrument declares:
- a durable identity, parent instrument/edition references, capabilities and compatibility version;
- a phonetic coverage map (languages, vowels, consonants, stress, melisma, ranges, articulation);
- an implementation/render backend (Alter/Ego compatibility, midi2sampler/formant, Csound, model-backed, physical or human performer);
- ownership and source licences (recorded contributors, voice consent, redistribution rules, text rights);
- a transport and timing profile (native MIDI2 UMP; declared lossy MIDI1 adaptation if needed);
- the supported input schema, expression controls, and witness/test receipts.

## The first family

| Working child | Sound idea | What makes it independently useful |
|---|---|---|
| **sing.syllable** | One voiced vowel with controllable initial/final consonants | Measures phonetic state, sustain, legato and releases. The minimal standalone test. |
| **sing.melisma** | A single vowel passes through multiple notes | Ensures Note On != automatically next syllable. |
| **sing.dialogue** | Two individually credited singing voices alternate dramatic lines | A Teatro score addresses performers independently. |
| **sing.choir** | Many voices with distinct pitch/timbre/timing states | Autonomous but bounded choral interpretation from one phrase plan. |
| **sing.archive.7vst** | Modern interpretive scores informed by original 7VST evidence | Keeps private archive, new rendition and published output separate. |
| **sing.witness** | A listener measures alignment, formants, discontinuity, latency | Records observed acoustic evidence, not an approval or an invented sound. |

These are **proposals**, not shipping software or registered MIDI-CI devices. New offspring may have a dedicated GitHub repository when they have an actual separately owned source, score/test surface or release. Avoid creating one repo per imagined feature.

## Bounded autonomy

An agent may propose phonetic readings, schedule notes, vary dynamics, rehearse a passage and generate *private candidate takes* under an explicit grant. It may not autonomously fabricate rights, spend money, access unrelated text, distribute singer models, rewrite canonical manuscripts, publish an edition or spawn unbounded agents. Parent/child relation is a versioned provenance edge, never a permissions escalation.

## Separation of products

- **sing**: reusable voice instrument + performance semantics.
- **Satzfunk**: author-directed, source-preserving studio, listening station and curated release.
- **Teatro**: staged scenes, ensemble/musical direction and orchestration.
- **Reframe**: work and editorial margins.
- **EstatePublisher**: release authority; neither an offspring nor a renderer may skip it.

One instrument may serve hundreds of Satzfunk works; one work may have many differently staged First Hearings. This is the intended proliferation, without duplicating an application or mixing its responsibilities.
