# 7VST: historical edition and a new instrument example

**7VST = historical Seven Against Thebes music / audiovisual project.** Its source edition is private and preserved independently of this public design work.

## What survives (evidence from the owning private repo)

[Satzfunk's historical restoration report](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/Satzfunk/blob/main/docs/archaeology/2026-10-21-7vst-original-performance-recovery.md) records:

- 18 original candidate WAV source exports, 18 numbered MP3 exports and 18 source MP4 video exports, independently hash-verified and held in private archives on the Mac external disk and Ubuntu; public Git does not contain their bytes.
- A 2017 Vuo preset-writer graph that manipulates ARIA phoneme sequencer XML and a Bidule test with 4,000 characters of dramatic text under the **Bones Basic** voice. Another Bidule uses **Daisy Star** for a distinct test. Text similarity to published parts 16/17 is verified, but **not** MIDI-to-lyric timing.
- Two separately recovered MIDI scores are real, but their route to the vocal performance is not proven.
- The original Studio One session and several autosaves remain iCloud placeholders; the precise historical project arrangement cannot yet be claimed as restored.
- Marie Ork is the **new** 2026 test voice and must not be relabelled as Daisy, Bones or the singer of the archived source.

The restored private [7VST edition](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/Satzfunk/blob/main/docs/archaeology/2026-10-21-7vst-restored-private-edition.md) joins 18 WAV/MP3/MP4 candidates with their published text entries in an authorized LAN browser; album order is not assumed to be dramaturgical chronology. Rights for the modern English translation, published credits and voice material need explicit review.

## Additional historical instrument: Wolfgang Palm's Phonem for iPad

The author reports **extensive use of PPG Phonem on iPad** and says it can be heard within the historical Unpopular Electronics Bandcamp material. This is important **author testimony**, distinct from the individually decoded Alter/Ego host states. The present archived session evidence does **not** establish which of the eighteen 7VST parts, or which other Bandcamp videos, contain Phonem. Do not present it as exclusive to 7VST or as an authenticated track-by-track attribution.

See [Phonem synthesis and archival evidence note](phonem-reference.md), [the private Satzfunk listening archive](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/Satzfunk/blob/main/archive/browser/index.html), and [the restored 7VST study browser](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/Satzfunk/blob/main/archive/browser/7vst-originals.html). A forensic listening review should save candidate track/timecode/edition/source hash before seeking independent iPad, session or stem corroboration.

## Four editions, not one mutable artifact

1. **Historical original:** unmodified 2017–2018 outputs and original authoring files; retain hashes and provenance, never overwrite.
2. **Historical study edition:** the private source-addressed 18-part browser and evidence ledger, clearly identified as a restoration for study.
3. **Alter/Ego contemporary reconstruction attempt:** a separate new, reproducible score and derived Plogue state created by sing's renderer-neutral model, using an available **licensed** voice and respecting any source text licence. No claim of original MIDI correspondence unless recovered.
4. **New sing/Teatro realization:** freely newly composed phonemes, note lines, harmonies, dramaturgy and acoustic material. Record it as an independent, versioned First Hearing candidate, to be accepted by the author and published only after rights review.

## 18-part design

Each part has a source ID, private source digest, lineation, optional voice(s), pronunciation plan, note timeline, render variants, and explicit release/rights state. Share voice models across parts; do not manufacture 18 separate embedded voice banks. The 18 Alter/Ego presets are an **output compatibility format**, not authoritative 7VST source truth.

```text
7vst/part-01 ... part-18 (source identity + provenance)
       ↘ phonetic plans + new scored performances (private derived editions)
         ↘ Alter/Ego AU state projection | native sing score
            ↘ verified listening takes
               ↘ author acceptance in Satzfunk → release eligibility
```

First prove a one-line synthetic phrase and one vocal renderer. Only afterwards define real historical lyric alignment using the privately verified originals and actual authorial decisions, never by guessing timings from track durations.

## Future children

A choir from 7VST's dramatic personae, a vocal percussion instrument, a one-syllable resonator, a reader-to-singer duet and a geographically situated Teatro performance may be separate sing offspring. They can inherit *contracts* and licensed source references, **not** implicitly inherit permission to reuse a copyrighted text, recording, or voice bank.
