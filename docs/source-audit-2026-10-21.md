## Conclusions

1. The reusable technical nucleus is MIDI2 + actual compiled midi2sampler DSP + a new explicit phonetic timeline; **nothing inspected provides that full combination**.
2. Alter/Ego and Marie Ork are external proprietary/reference renderers. Keep their licensed binaries and 2017 assets outside this repo.
3. Contexter/Chormusik is worth preserving as a conceptual predecessor; it is **not** proof that earlier Apple speech synthesis generated singing.
4. No deployment, binary compilation or audible cross-repository integration was executed during this source audit. New acceptance tests must independently verify each candidate.
